FAQ

Zanmi is a platform that connects locals and expats in Toulouse to share their cultures and enjoy unique experiences throughout the city. Whether you're visiting or a resident, Zanmi offers events that combine discovery, socializing, and enriching encounters.

Zanmi offers a wide variety of events to suit all tastes: social outings, cultural activities, sports and wellness, and themed meetups. Every event is designed to create memorable moments tailored to participants' preferences.

Events on the platform are paid to cover management costs, reward organizers, and ensure a quality experience. However, Zanmi provides a WhatsApp group where everyone can organize free and spontaneous meetups.

Joining an event is simple:
1. Sign up on the platform by creating an account.
2. Browse the list of available events and select one that interests you.
3. Click "Join," complete the secure payment, and wait for the organizer's confirmation.
4. You will then receive all the necessary details (location, time, etc.) to attend the event.

Once registered, get ready to have an amazing time with other participants!

Events are organized by carefully selected individuals. Each organizer is chosen for their experience, reliability, and passion for creating unique and welcoming moments. Zanmi also supports these organizers to ensure well-planned and inclusive events.

Participant safety is a priority. All organizers must verify their identity and meet strict criteria before hosting an event. The venues chosen for meetups are public, accessible, and known for their safety. For women who prefer, gender-specific events are available, allowing them to join female-only gatherings.
